🌳 How to play Greedy Growers

  1. Buy a seed at the river. Open the Shop, pick a seed bag, and buy it.
  2. Plant it. Your seed bags appear at the bottom of the garden. Tap a bag, then tap an empty plot.
  3. Watch it grow. Trees grow taller and more valuable over time. The price tag above each tree shows what it's worth right now.
  4. Harvest before the lightning. ⛈️ Storms roll in with a warning. Tap a tree to harvest it — waiting too long means lightning turns your fortune to charcoal.

💡 Greed streak: harvest a tree at 60%+ growth to stack 🔥 (up to +50% value). Harvesting too early resets it.

💡 Lightning rods reduce strike odds. Fully grown trees are the tastiest target for Zeus.

💡 Codes: try ILOVECATS for a starter boost.
